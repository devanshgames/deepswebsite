Founded in Bhopal, MP in 1993, Deepa Hosiery began with a singular vision: to bring uncompromising quality to the wholesale lingerie market.

We believe that true comfort is engineered. That's why every piece of fabric is carefully deconstructed in our design phase, and every finished garment is rigorously inspected before it leaves our facility.

For over three decades, we haven't just been manufacturing apparel; we've been crafting confidence. Our commitment to B2B excellence means your retail business receives products that guarantee customer satisfaction and repeat business.

Questions & Answers.

Everything you need to know about partnering with us.

As a B2B wholesale provider, our standard MOQ is designed to support retail businesses of various sizes. Please contact our sales team through the Partner Portal for specific volume requirements tailored to your business.

All Deepa Hosiery products are proudly conceptualized, deconstructed, inspected, and crafted at our facilities in Bhopal, MP. We have maintained strict quality control here since 1993.

Currently, we focus exclusively on providing the premium Deepa Hosiery brand to our retail partners to ensure consistent brand quality and recognition.

Our video states it best: 'Deconstructed. Inspected.' We perform a multi-stage quality check on every batch. Threads are tested for tensile strength, and finished garments undergo rigorous sizing and defect inspections before B2B dispatch.

Standard dispatch from our Bhopal facility takes 3-5 business days upon order confirmation. Delivery times vary based on your location within India.
